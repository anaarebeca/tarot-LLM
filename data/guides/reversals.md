# Reversals guide

## What a reversed card means

A reversed card does not mean the opposite of the upright card. It shows the same energy in a different state: blocked, delayed, excessive, or turned inward. Read the reversed meaning given for the card, and use the position to decide which of these fits. A reversed card in the Past position often describes something that was avoided or left unfinished.

## Blocked or delayed energy

Many reversals describe an energy that wants to move but is being held back. The change, opportunity or feeling is still present, but fear, timing or circumstances slow it down. When a card shows delay, the reading should say what is being postponed and invite the person to look at what is holding it back, without predicting that it will never happen.

## Internal rather than external

A reversed card often moves the theme from the outside world to the inside. An upright card may show an event, while the reversed one shows how the person feels about it or handles it privately. Use this when a card sits in a position about emotions, hopes or fears: the reversed meaning usually points to an inner process.

## Excess, or too little

Some reversals show too much of a quality, others too little. Reversed Strength may mean self-doubt, while a reversed Emperor may mean rigidity or lack of control. Check the keywords of the reversed meaning to see which direction applies, and describe it as something to balance, not as a judgment.

## Many reversed cards in one spread

When most of the cards in a spread are reversed, the situation is likely stalled, unresolved or mostly internal. Mention the overall pattern before going through the positions one by one. A single reversed card among upright ones highlights that position as the point of tension or the lesson of the spread.

## Reversed Major Arcana

A reversed Major Arcana card points to a large theme that is being resisted, delayed or processed slowly, such as an avoided change or an unfinished lesson. Because Major cards carry weight, say clearly which life area is affected in the reading, using the position and the person's context.
