# Suits guide

## Wands
Fire. Energy, ambition, creativity and action. Many Wands in a spread point to a
phase of drive and new projects. Too few can mean low motivation.

## Cups
Water. Emotions, relationships and intuition. Many Cups suggest emotional depth, meaningful connections, or a focus on relationships. Too few can indicate emotional distance, difficulty expressing feelings, or neglecting your emotional needs.

## Swords
Air. Intellect, communication, logic and conflict. Many Swords point to intense mental activity, important decisions, challenges, or disagreements. Too few can suggest avoiding difficult conversations, unclear thinking, or struggling to communicate.

## Pentacles
Earth. Material matters, finances, career, stability and physical well-being. Many Pentacles suggest a focus on financial security, professional growth, or building something lasting. Too few can indicate instability, financial concerns, or neglecting practical responsibilities.
